## Required guard

An assistant may autonomously reconcile factual status, but it must retain statement-level provenance and may not promote a proposal into authority. A user correction invalidates dependent assistant-authored statements until the whole goal and every downstream edit are re-audited. Test replacement and test retirement are behavior-level decisions: mutable status cannot invent a replacement test merely to close a ledger row for a removed architecture.
