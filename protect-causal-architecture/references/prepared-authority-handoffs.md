# Prepared Authority Handoffs

Load this reference when parsing, migration, normalization, planning, or compilation gives a prepared result authority over a field that downstream orchestration consumes. It applies to that handoff and its effects, not ordinary conversions with no authority epoch.

## Follow the field owner

Identify the raw representation, preparation owner, canonical prepared accessor or typed field, first consumer, and affected later consumers. After successful preparation, obtain the canonically interpreted field from the prepared result. Do not repeat legacy precedence, migration fallbacks, or inference from stale raw input in orchestration.

Retain raw input for independently owned facts that preparation does not interpret. A prepared object does not become the owner of every field merely because it exists. Preserve attribution of the selected value through the affected calls, typed observations, outcomes, and authorized persistence.

If field ownership remains unresolved, stop only the dependent effect and resolve that owner. An exact change already approved by the user needs no renewed approval, matrix, or audit packet.

## Name the protected effect boundary

Identify the relevant validation or selection barrier and the target writes or mutation phase it protects, together with affected cleanup, recovery, and persistence obligations. Preserve the selected ordering when changing the authority read; do not move validation after those effects, persist authority early, or bypass recovery.

Authorized preparation may already have changed files, tool state, or external systems before the protected target phase. Account for those earlier effects and their actual recovery obligations. Failure before target mutation does not prove that preparation was effect-free, that restoration never ran, or that an inventory transaction rolled back external commands.

Keep the affected edge and its counterfactual in the existing task, plan, goal, or code contract. Expand to a larger chain only when interacting consumers or phases would otherwise remain ambiguous. This reference does not require a separate durable record.

## Exercise reachable workflow behavior

- For valid legacy or incomplete input that preparation canonicalizes, exercise the production preparation and complete workflow. Observe the prepared value at the first and affected later consumers and in the typed outcome; engine-unit success alone does not establish the handoff.
- Add an unresolved-authority negative only when a constructible production input can reach that failure. Remove the legitimate authority sources relevant to that case, assert the existing typed failure at its declared boundary, and prove that the named protected target writes or mutation phase did not begin.
- If production always derives or defaults a valid value, preserve and test that supported behavior. Do not invent a missing-authority branch, fabricate an impossible prepared state, or widen a public constructor or API solely to reach a test seam.
- Preserve any legitimate earlier preparation effects and required cleanup or recovery in the negative observation. Do not turn a target-write guard into an assertion that every earlier effect or restoration attempt was absent.
- Use role-correct inputs through the production boundary, preserving any explicit fixture identity or starting-byte requirements. Assert field ownership and causal effects through typed or owner-level observations.

Use the existing implementation and verification authority. Describing the evidence does not authorize execution, persistence, a new ledger, or a change to a settled test disposition.
