# Contract-reference variant

## Concrete intent

Turn location-independent parsed-source transformation requirements into a compact adoption and review checklist.

## Approach

Keep semantic identity, discovery, drift predicates, syntax anchoring, minimal rewrite, typed classification, transaction order, postcondition, replay, and atomic publication distinct. Treat paths, markers, versions, rendered bodies, hashes, and fingerprints as hints or diagnostics only.

## Preserved nuance

Exact whole-content replacement remains legitimate only for explicitly repository-owned generated or vendored content. Schema or checklist completion is not semantic proof.

## Relationships and uncertainty

This overlaps `$design-semantic-source-transforms`, `$protect-causal-architecture`, and `$audit-architectural-regressions`. Review should decide whether the compact checklist adds value beyond merging it into the official design owner.
