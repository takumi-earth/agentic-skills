# Legacy-migration-inventory variant

## Concrete intent

Produce a stable review ledger of transformation callers whose targeting or evidence may depend on fixed paths, marker gates, complete bodies, fingerprints, hashes, regexes, or textual fallbacks.

## Approach

Use deterministic static signals to emit site keys, locations, signal classes, candidate owners, dispositions, and uncertainty without rewriting source or treating a match as proof of a defect.

## Preserved nuance

Inventory signals are review leads. They must never become transformation applicability predicates or authorize remediation.

## Relationships and uncertainty

This overlaps `$audit-architectural-regressions` and complements the contract variants. Review should decide whether language-specific adapters belong in siblings rather than broadening the generic scanner.
